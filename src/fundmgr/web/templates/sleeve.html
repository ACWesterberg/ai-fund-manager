ⓘ How monitoring works

Alerts push to Telegram. Kill lines are checked against prices every run — a breach pushes once, then re-arms when the position recovers. The distance to a kill line is measured from the review price when one is set, otherwise from cost.

Text criteria are judged daily against news summaries, article text and cached fundamentals; one marked not auto-checkable needs figures only the report carries, so it's quoted back to you at each print instead.

Horizons nudge at 30 / 14 / 7 / 1 days and on the day, as a prompt to re-run the thesis. Drift ≥ 1.50× target fires the rebalance alert; the weight bar ends at that threshold and the tick marks the target.

An add signal needs (proof or dislocation) and valuation and weight, unless the kill criterion is active. A price fall alone is never a kill — it makes the evidence more urgent to re-read.

Verify every alert before acting.
